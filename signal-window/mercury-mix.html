Two decks. One fader. Either hand.

The key rows are alternative hand positions for the same crossfader. Select a deck separately to work its platter or transport.

Sample pads
Hold an empty pad while the selected deck plays or scratches. Release to keep up to eight seconds; a quick tap captures a short slice. Tap a loaded pad to replay it; Shift-click clears it. Pads capture that deck before EQ/effects, and playback goes into the master mix and recording. Samples stay in this browser, separately from saved set files. Stop pads stops playback and finishes any current capture.
Scratch moves mode
Select a deck with 1/2, mark a sound, then tap or hold A S D F G H J K L ; Q W. Each pad plays a named motion/cut phrase from that cue. Tap plays one cycle; hold repeats. New keys replace the active phrase. Escape or Stop moves cancels. These are playable interpretations of scratch techniques, not a claim to reproduce a human turntablist’s articulation.
Manual fader mode · A S D F / J K L ;
Four fader positions. Roll across adjacent keys to sweep; overlapping keys blend their positions.

Drag the record
Grab and scratch forward or backward. Release to resume. Touchscreens can grab both decks at once.
Scroll over a record
Briefly nudge its speed. Hold T to grab the selected platter, then scroll to scratch. Physical trackpad finger zones are not exposed by ordinary browsers.
Z / X · R · V
Nudge the selected deck slower/faster · reverse · toggle slip. Slip catches up to where playback would have been after a scratch.
Headphones / preview
Listen A/B taps each deck before the crossfader. Cue → main mix blends that preview with the master. Choose a separate output device for private cueing where supported. Preview here replaces the audible main output; split cable puts mono main on left and mono cue on right. Recordings always contain the stereo master only.
On key release
Stay where I leave it latches the fader. Return to A or B springs back to that deck when all fader keys are released. Silence selected deck closes whichever deck is selected with 1/2.
Cue / Sync / Slip / Dub space
Cue pauses and returns to the main cue, set with Set beat 1. Sync matches the other deck’s speed and beat phase. Slip rejoins the uninterrupted timeline after scratching. Dub space adds a filtered repeating echo and reverb; tap again to disengage.
Hot cues 1–4
Tap an empty pad to set it; tap a filled pad to jump and play. Shift-click clears a pad, or use Clear cues.

BPM detection is an estimate. Edit it, tap a tempo, and set Beat 1 to correct the grid. Sync matches playback speed and aligns the grid; changing speed also changes pitch, like vinyl.

Record exports what you mix on this device. Saved sets keep deck references, cues and controls; local audio files must be selected again after reopening. No local files or recordings are uploaded automatically.

Sound length selects more audio and gives it more time. Scratch pace: left for gentler, longer strokes; right for sharper, faster strokes. Changes apply to the next phrase; song playback stays unchanged.

Moves use the selected track’s real audio at its marked cue, with timed record motion and cuts. They add that deck over the current mix while held; releasing finishes the phrase and restores your fader. Tempo starts from the other playing deck. Manual fader mode keeps ASDF / JKL;.
